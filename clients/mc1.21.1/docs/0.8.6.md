# 0.8.6 - ranged weapons and combat

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.248, Java 21. Client-side only.
This is the next test build in the ranged pass, not a newer-Minecraft port.

## What changed

- Mob Bow Aim becomes **Mob Ranged Aim | 몹 원거리 조준**.
- Player Bow Aim becomes **Player Ranged Aim | 플레이어 원거리 조준**.
- The two independently selectable target categories now share one implementation.
  When both are enabled, only one controller picks across the allowed categories;
  two aim routines no longer turn the camera toward competing targets each tick.
- Aim uses a bounded low-arc intercept calculation with vanilla arrow/trident
  air drag, gravity, optional target motion and bow/trident shooter momentum.
- Stable target selection and frame-rate-independent easing replace fixed tick jumps.
- Bow Status HUD becomes **Ranged Status HUD | 원거리 상태 HUD**, retaining its
  old config ID, enabled state and saved position. One panel serves all three weapons.
- The misleading nearest-target straight-line ETA has been removed. The panel now
  reports actual weapon charge, available arrows, loaded crossbow or trident readiness.
  Text and percentage have their own areas; the bar sits below both text rows.
- Ranged settings have their own right-click screen. The existing general Options
  range/speed sliders are relabeled and edit the same values, not duplicate settings.

## Enable and use

1. Back up config/gumiho.json and keep your old JAR outside the mods folder.
   Replace it with gumiho-neoforge-0.8.6.jar; do not enable two GUMIHO JARs.
2. Open GUMIHO with Left Shift (or your custom menu key). In Combat, enable
   Mob Ranged Aim, Player Ranged Aim, or both. Their saved old toggles still work.
3. Right-click either aim card or Ranged Status HUD for weapon and prediction settings.
4. Bows are enabled in weapon settings by default. Crossbows and thrown tridents
   default OFF so an upgrade does not silently enable assistance for new weapons.

| Weapon | When assistance works | Fire control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bow | While drawing with sufficient charge and ammo | Release Use yourself |
| Arrow crossbow | While loading; after loading, hold the Ranged Aim key (V by default) | Click Use yourself |
| Thrown trident | While holding Use after the native 10-tick charge threshold | Release Use yourself |
| Riptide trident | Excluded from aim assistance; HUD identifies it | Fully manual |
| Firework crossbow | Excluded from aim assistance; HUD identifies it | Fully manual |

V is remappable under Minecraft Controls > GUMIHO > Hold Loaded Crossbow Aim.
Hold it before firing a loaded crossbow; pressing it and shooting simultaneously
does not promise time to acquire/turn toward a target. Releasing V stops loaded
crossbow assistance. It does not load, fire or consume ammunition.

Main/offhand use is recognized. When holding an idle main-hand ranged weapon it
takes precedence over an idle offhand one. An offhand shield being used is not
misidentified as a ranged weapon. Active offhand ranged aiming suppresses competing
main-hand melee automation; it does not otherwise change melee cooldown/mace rules.

## Settings and boundaries

- Weapon toggles: Bow ON; Crossbow OFF; Thrown Trident OFF.
- Target Lead ON: predicts constant current target movement, not future decisions.
- Check Flight Path ON: rejects predicted paths blocked by terrain or another
  pickable entity. Water/other fluid endpoints and unloaded endpoints also reject
  the air-only model. OFF skips these path checks, not direct visibility/friend rules.
- Range: 16-96m, preserving the previous bow range (64m default).
- Full FOV: 10-180 degrees, default 70. The global target FOV can narrow it further.
- Aim Speed: 0.5-10 degrees/tick, preserving the old bow speed (3.5 default).
- Aim Ease: 50-400ms, default 100. Lower is snappier, higher is softer.
- Global target range, hostile/passive toggles, marked enemies, pin priority,
  invisibility setting and teammate/neutral exclusions still apply.
- Active aim is still server-allowlisted, stops in menus/on focus loss/when paused,
  and participates in End-key Emergency Disable. No new packet protocol or server
  component was added. HUD and Projectile Paths remain informational features.

## Projectile Paths: whose shots?

The 0.8.5 Utility module remains available in 0.8.6. It includes supported shots
from **you, other players (including teammates), and mobs** such as skeletons,
ghasts and breezes. My Projectiles and Other Projectiles are separate filters.
Unknown owners count as Other. The colors represent projectile types, not teams.
Only loaded, initially visible projectiles within the configured range/count are
selected; this does not track every shot anywhere on the server.

## In-game acceptance checklist

Test in a disposable world first, then your exact approved-server modpack:

1. Confirm 0.8.6 in the menu/HUD; prior module toggles and HUD positions survive.
2. Bow: aim at a stationary hostile mob at 10/25/40m. Compare half/full draw;
   then try a sideways-moving mob. The camera should ease without sudden target
   swaps. Releasing Use shoots once through normal gameplay, never automatically.
3. Enable both aim cards with a mob and marked enemy in view. Only one target
   should be followed at a time. Disable each card in turn to check category scope.
4. Mark a test player as teammate/neutral: no direct targeting. Put that player
   in the predicted flight path to another target: with path checking ON, aiming
   should pause. This cannot guarantee safety if someone moves into a shot later.
5. Crossbow: enable its setting. Check loading with normal and Quick Charge
   crossbows. After loading, hold V, aim, and click Use. Release V: no camera pull.
   Repeat with a loaded firework: HUD identifies it and aim stays manual.
6. Trident: enable its setting, hold Use for at least ten ticks, aim and release.
   Test a Loyalty trident returning normally; no return-flight automation is added.
   Test Riptide in water/rain: HUD identifies it and the module must not steer you.
7. Test offhand bow/crossbow/trident plus a main-hand melee weapon: no competing
   melee steering or auto-attacks during active ranged aiming.
8. Try no ammo, depleted trident durability, target behind wall, menu open,
   Alt-Tab/focus loss, dimension change and unallowlisted server: no unintended aim.
9. Check HUD percent/bar separation at your normal GUI scale; drag it in the HUD
   editor, switch weapons, and restart to check settings/layout persistence.
10. Recheck existing mace combo, wind-charge swap, shield release and full-charge
    melee timing. Projectile Paths should still show your shots AND mob shots.

## Limits / verification

This predicts an airborne low arc with constant target movement and no random
spread. It does not guarantee hits, critical damage, future enemy movement or
network timing. Speeds outside the supported envelope, unreachable targets and
shots requiring over 80 ticks are rejected. Water transitions, modded physics,
multishot side arrows, fireworks, Riptide and future native spear support require
separate work. No automatic firing/release, auto-reload, held-item trajectory
preview, inventory management or new server component is included.

Automated checks exercise intercept solutions against forward flight integration,
weapon activation gates, controller arbitration, config preservation and the
existing combat/projectile suites. Compilation verifies the 1.21.1 NeoForge APIs.
Live hit accuracy, aiming feel, shader/mod compatibility and FPS need in-game testing.
