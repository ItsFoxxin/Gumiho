# 0.9.2 - Consistent HUD surfaces

Paired maintenance release for Minecraft 1.21.1 and 26.2, based on 0.9.1.
No new combat, movement, networking or config behavior.

## Changes
- Every HUD panel uses the same warm near-black background (#120E0B) and opacity (208/255, about 82%).
- Removes the gameplay HUD pass underneath HUD Editor previews. Enabled and disabled previews now render once each, avoiding extra darkening and blurred duplicate edges.
- Survival heading and side stripe are orange; alert text and detection remain unchanged.
- Coordinates now have a matching background, orange stripe, and text padding.
- No positions, per-panel sizes, toggles, presets, or permissions are reset.
- Other alert accents and progress-bar tracks retain their distinct colors.

Transparency still lets different parts of the world show through. Deliberately overlapping two separate panels also layers their backgrounds; the editor no longer renders the same panel twice.

## Install and check
Keep the old JAR outside mods for rollback, then install just the matching 0.9.2 JAR.
Leave config/gumiho.json and config/gumiho-setups intact. No reset or preset re-import is needed.

Check in both game builds:
1. Open HUD Editor and reveal OFF panels; enabled and disabled panels should have the same surface treatment.
2. Drag/resize a panel and confirm no darker ghost remains at its gameplay position.
3. Confirm Survival is orange and coordinates fit their new box. The box adds padding, so a panel at the right edge may be clamped inward to fit without changing its stored anchor.
4. Close the editor; confirm the normal HUD returns once, with the saved positions and sizes.
5. Check the HUD against bright/dark terrain and with your shaders.

## Validation boundary
Automated palette coverage, single-pass render ownership, startup, HUD sizing, preset, combat and utility regressions are included.
In-game appearance, shader interaction and modpack acceptance still require a live check.
The Elytra Takeoff and Flight Status modules are in the separate 0.9.5 development source, not this maintenance release.
GUMIHO Link is not implemented by this release; 1.0.0 still requires Link integration and end-to-end acceptance.
